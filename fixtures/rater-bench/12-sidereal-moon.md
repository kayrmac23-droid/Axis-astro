## The Moon

Pisces holds in both frames, so this is the same sign read for different territory: Tropical placed this Moon in the fifth house, where dissolution poured into romance and creative absorption; the Sidereal Moon sits in the sixth, where Pisces is a neutral sign to the Moon — neither helped nor strained by status, running at its own native permeability with no dignity to answer for. What changes is not the feeling but where it has to go to be useful: the same boundarylessness that made a creative project or an early romance feel like merger now has to operate inside work, health and the daily maintenance of the body, where dissolving the edge between yourself and what is near you is a very different proposition.

House 6 is a difficult house, and it asks something specific of this Moon: your sense of being emotionally safe gets tested not in who you love but in whether the routine is sustainable, whether the body is being listened to, whether service to others has a limit you can actually hold. The same absorption that once looked like devotion in a creative or romantic register now shows up as difficulty distinguishing your own fatigue from the demands around you — work and the care of others expand to fill whatever boundary Pisces fails to supply.

The lordship carried in matters here: this Moon rules House 10, Cancer, so public standing and vocation ride into House 6 on the back of emotional instinct rather than ambition. What you do for reputation and what you do out of felt obligation are not two separate projects; they are the same impulse, which means your professional footing is more emotionally load-bearing than it looks from outside, and strain at work registers in the body before it registers as a complaint.

The conjunction with Jupiter, domicile in this same sign and house, gives the dissolving instinct real scope rather than drift — Jupiter's Harsha Yoga confines a difficulty-house lord to its own domain, so the overextension this Moon is prone to here has somewhere to be converted into competence rather than simply absorbed and lost. The conjunction with Rahu, close in the same sign, pulls differently: it is the same hunger the Tropical reading named in the fifth house, now operating on routine and service rather than on a person or a project — a task or an obligation can take on an inflated, consuming weight that outruns what it is actually worth, and the sixth house gives it a sanctioned place to do so under the cover of diligence. The seventh-house drishti from Mars, cast from Virgo in the twelfth, lands here as a demand for discipline arriving from a will that itself works privately and late — so the correction this Moon needs often arrives already deferred, after the absorption has gone further than it would have with earlier intervention.

The Ketu mahadasha, running as disengagement, has made withdrawal from exactly this kind of obligatory entanglement its long project; the Mercury antardasha, only weeks old, turns that same scrutiny inward, onto how the body and the routine have actually been bearing the weight. Uttara Bhadrapada, ruled by Saturn and presided over by Ahir Budhnya, names the instinct underneath all of this plainly: a capacity for sustained depth and patient containment that this Moon can mistake for health, holding a punishing routine or an exhausting obligation for far longer than the body or the task can actually justify, because containment and endurance look, from the inside, exactly alike.
