## The Sun

Swati's theme is movement that bends without breaking, and under Rahu's rulership the instinct it adds to this Sun is a restlessness no sign description would predict: even seated, settled, apparently at rest, some part of you is testing whether the current position is still the right one, ready to pivot the moment it feels fixed. Vayu presides, not as decoration but as the governing image — wind does not hold a shape, it moves through whatever shape it meets. This Sun carries that quality as an undertone to everything else about it.

The sign itself has moved back one position from the Tropical reading, Scorpio to Libra, same first house. Where Scorpio Sun made identity a matter of depth and merger, Libra Sun makes it a matter of calibration — the instinctive measure you take of a room is not "what is hidden here" but "has this settled into balance yet," and you register a situation as unfinished business until it has. In Jyotish terms this placement is debilitated, Libra sitting opposite the Sun's exaltation, and the strain is specific: asserting plain selfhood costs more effort here than it would in almost any other sign, because Libra's fairness instinct keeps checking your own standing against everyone else's before it will let you claim anything as simply yours.

A classical condition softens the picture without erasing it — Mars, ruler of the Sun's exaltation sign, sits angularly from the Moon, which meets the formal test for cancelling the debilitation. Still conjunct Mercury and Pluto in the same sign and house, your sense of self keeps returning to the same unsettled question rather than resting in it, Pluto's intensity pressed against a Sun already working to stand upright.

The lordship carried in is H11's — community, networks, the collective you belong to — so what gets processed daily at the threshold of identity is your place among others, not merely your own form. The Ketu mahadasha has run as disengagement from exactly that network pressure; the Mercury antardasha, only weeks old, turns the processing inward, bringing retrograde Mercury's private revision to bear directly on a Sun occupying its own house. The chapter asks less that you resolve what Libra cannot settle than that you notice, this month, how much of your identity has been waiting on other people's verdict before it would call itself finished.
