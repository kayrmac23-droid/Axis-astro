## The Lunar Nodes

Ketu sits in Libra in your twelfth house, the same house that already holds Mars and Mercury, which means the fairness and diplomatic ease this chart has mastered is a skill you exercise almost entirely out of view. You know how to weigh a disagreement until it balances, how to see both sides before either has finished speaking — Mars's own placement has already shown you this habit at work in the will itself. Ketu's shadow is what that mastery costs: a fluency in withdrawal that lets you disengage from a conflict cleanly, see through to its essence, and never quite commit to standing inside it. The twelfth house means this release happens privately, so what looks like composure is often just practised departure.

Rahu sits in Aries in your sixth house, conjunct your Moon and trine your Venus, both close — the nodal axis runs directly through the two placements that already govern how you attach. Where Ketu lets go cleanly, Rahu wants direct, unmediated assertion in the daily work of the body and routine: starting the improved regimen now, saying the want plainly instead of weighing it first. The Moon conjunction means this hunger arrives fused with your deepest emotional instinct, not separate from it, and the Venus trine means desire moves toward it without friction, so the pull toward Aries can inflate into wanting most intensely exactly where you have practised balance longest. The square to Neptune keeps this directness perpetually blurred by what you have absorbed from elsewhere; the wide square to Uranus means it also arrives without warning. What you are actually choosing, each time, is between the fluency of stepping back and the risk of saying the thing before you have finished weighing it.
