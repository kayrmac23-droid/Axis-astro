## Mercury

Mercury stays in Libra across both frames, so the sign evidence the Tropical reading already drew — balance as the steering instinct, Venus in Scorpio in detriment as its dispositor — holds. What moves is the house: Tropical placed this Mercury twelfth, weighing a conversation alone before it ever reached a room; the Sidereal Lagna puts it in House 1, and the houses it rules there give that weighing somewhere concrete to answer for. Mercury rules the ninth and the twelfth from this Lagna and sits in the first, so belief and private process both ride into the body and the instinctive persona — what you think you're allowed to say out loud is governed, daily, by convictions you've only half-examined and by material you have deliberately kept from view.

Mercury sits in Libra as a friend's sign, Venus's own rulership offering cognitive comfort without the authority dignity would add — the diplomatic instinct is native, not effortful, but it carries no extra weight of its own. Chitra, this placement's nakshatra, is ruled by Mars and presided over by Vishvakarma, the architect-deity, and it adds something the sign alone does not: a drive toward making a thought into a finished, well-formed thing, not just a balanced one. Your mind doesn't only want fairness between two readings of an event; it wants the sentence that finally gets the shape of it right, the way a craftsman wants the join to disappear.

Mercury sits conjunct Sun and Pluto, both in Libra in House 1 — the same fused intensity the Sidereal Sun section already named, now pulling your thinking rather than your identity into its orbit. A thought here doesn't stay a thought; it gets charged with the stakes of selfhood itself, so revising a position can feel like revising who you are. Mercury casts its own drishti forward into House 7, Aries, currently untenanted — nowhere for that scrutiny to land outside you, no partner or adversary absorbing the weighing on your behalf. It returns to you entirely, which is why the retrograde habit of private revision rarely finds an external correction and has to complete itself alone.
