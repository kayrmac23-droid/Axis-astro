## Mars

From the sixth house, Mars looks straight at Moon, Jupiter and Rahu — its own seventh-house drishti landing squarely on the absorption the Sidereal Moon section already described, the task or obligation that swells past what it's worth. What this drishti actually adds is correction with nowhere easy to land: Mars presses discipline and limit onto a Moon-Jupiter-Rahu cluster that specialises in not noticing when it has taken on too much, and that pressure is coming from a will that has its own trouble acting cleanly. Mars casts further into Sagittarius, H3, reaching Uranus and Neptune, and backward into Aries, H7, where nothing stands to receive it — an empty house, so the demand for direct confrontation this drishti would otherwise deliver to a partnership has no target and simply dissipates.

Mars has moved back one sign from the Tropical reading, Libra to Virgo, the house unchanged at twelfth. Where Libra Mars deferred and weighed, asking a relational sign to carry a warrior's urgency, Virgo Mars works by correction and refinement instead — the same withheld assertion, now applied to getting a thing precisely right rather than to brokering agreement. Hasta's theme is exact craftsmanship, and under Pada 4, ruled by the Moon and presided over by Savitar, the skill this placement wants is not conceptual but made: your hands, your actual labour, fixing what is wrong with something until the fix disappears into the work.

Virgo is Mercury's sign, and Mercury is Mars's natural enemy — this is enemy's-sign placement, not detriment but close to it functionally, meaning the precision Virgo offers Mars is a tool it cannot wield with full confidence. The drive to act gets channelled into refining and perfecting rather than striking, and something in that channel resents the substitution even while using it. Mars rules H2 and H7 from this Lagna and sits in H12, so your resources and your open partnerships both get decided through private, invisible labour rather than through anything declared — what you're worth and what you'd confront someone about both get settled alone, first, in a sign that would rather correct the draft one more time than send it.

The next time something genuinely needs confronting, this sign status is what decides how the confrontation arrives: not refused, not avoided, but revised into its most defensible form before anyone else sees a version of it. Enemy's sign means Mars can still finish the job — Virgo's precision is real — but it finishes alone, correcting itself against a standard only you are holding, and the thing that reaches another person is already the corrected copy, not the original complaint.
