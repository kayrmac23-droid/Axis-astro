## Living the Divergence

Neither reading tells this person which question to ask at the threshold of a room, and both questions get asked every time. The Tropical body arrives probing for what is concealed; the Sidereal body arrives checking whether the exchange has settled into something even. Both operations route through the twelfth house before either produces a word, which means the posture that finally reaches the room is a single face built to answer two separate interrogations at once — and the person wearing it has no way to know, afterwards, which question actually got answered, because only one answer ever comes out.

The Mars divergence makes this a daily cost rather than an occasional one. A detriment Mars deferring for consensus and a peregrine Mars correcting for precision both withhold the same confrontation, for different reasons, in the same hidden house — so when something finally needs to be said, there is no telling, from the outside or from within, whether what gets delivered has been smoothed for fairness or polished for accuracy. Either way it arrives already finished, and the labour that finished it leaves no trace to consult.

The Sun divergence refuses even that much symmetry. A peregrine Scorpio Sun pays no structural tax for its intensity; a debilitated Libra Sun pays one constantly, straining to hold ordinary selfhood before Pluto's weight even lands. One frame explains the density as simply what this person is. The other insists the same density costs something to carry every time it shows up, and offers no account of when the bill comes due.
