## Rahu and Ketu

The frame shift carries this axis back one sign on each end — Rahu from Aries into Pisces, Ketu from Libra into Virgo — with both houses holding steady, sixth and twelfth. What the Tropical reading named as a fused, Moon-conjunct hunger for direct assertion in the body and routine now sits in the sign it has to dissolve into rather than announce, and the whole character of the craving changes with it.

Rahu in Pisces conjuncts both Moon and Jupiter in the sixth house, and this is a different hunger from the one Aries would produce: not the drive to start the improved system now, but an appetite that wants to be absorbed completely into service, routine, the body's daily demands, without ever quite locating an edge where enough has been given. Uttara Bhadrapada's theme is patient depth and containment, presided over by Ahir Budhnya, the serpent of the deep — and under Saturn's rulership of the nakshatra, the hunger this Rahu carries wants to look like discipline. It takes on the shape of someone who simply works harder, holds more, endures longer, when what is actually happening is desire wearing the sixth house's respectable clothing. Mars casts its drishti here from Virgo in the twelfth, a demand for limit arriving from a will that itself defers — so the correction this placement needs rarely lands before the absorption has already gone past what the task was worth.

Ketu in Virgo sits with Mars in the twelfth house, and Uttara Phalguni's theme — reliability, covenant, fair dealing, under the Sun's rulership and Aryaman's presidency — names a capacity for sustained, honourable commitment that costs this placement nothing to produce; it is already finished business, exercised privately, in the house where nothing gets declared. The drishti it receives from Moon and Jupiter, both reaching across from Pisces, brings exactly the sixth-house material Rahu is hungry for into contact with the house that no longer needs to prove it can commit.

What this axis asks you to release is not service itself but the belief that precision and improvement are what your worth is made of — Virgo's gifts, already available without strain, held too tightly because letting the standard soften feels like letting yourself go slack. What it asks you to grow into is a hunger that has nowhere glamorous to express, only the unglamorous arena of routine and the body, where the task is to notice when devotion to the work has quietly become indistinguishable from an inability to stop.
