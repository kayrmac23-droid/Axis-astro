## The Sun

You keep yourself movable. When a group, an alliance or a set of terms starts closing around you, you bend toward it far enough to stay inside it and no further, and you tend to leave a door open that nobody else noticed you were holding. That instinct comes from Swati, the nakshatra your Sun occupies. Swati is ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind, and it names a self that stays well by staying in motion. In this frame the Sun moves back one sign, from Scorpio to Libra, while the house stays the same: the first, in both charts.

### The Sun in Libra, House 1

Libra is the Sun's sign of debilitation (neecha, the sign where a planet works furthest from its natural strength), and in the first house you feel it in your body and your bearing. Your will reaches you already shaped by whoever is present, so a clear "this is what I want" tends to arrive only after you've measured it against someone else's position. That's a standing cost. Saying what you want first, before you know how it lands, takes more out of you than the moment seems to justify, and sometimes you don't say it at all. One classical condition softens the debilitation: Mars, lord of Aries where the Sun is exalted, sits in an angle from your Moon. It gives you access to a firmer self-assertion when something pushes hard enough, and the everyday effort it costs you to state a preference stays where it is.

The Sun rules your eleventh house, the house of gains, friendships and the groups you belong to, and it brings all of that into the first. So the circles you move in become part of how you carry yourself, and your sense of who you are shifts with the company. This is the part of life where the pattern runs: in networks, shared ventures, and any setting where you are one voice among several and the terms are still open. It rests when you're working alone on something with no group to answer to. There, the Swati movability has nothing to bend toward, and the self steadies.

Mercury and Pluto share the sign with the Sun. Mercury, retrograde, keeps you going back over a decision you've already announced, revising your own account of what you want. Pluto adds a hard core under the flexibility, a point past which you won't bend, and you usually find it only when someone presses on it. Your Sun's gaze falls on an empty seventh house in Aries. Your attention keeps going toward partnership, and no planet there gives anything back to measure yourself against.

Venus, the Sun's dispositor, sits in Scorpio in your second house, in detriment. The self you show in company is therefore governed by a ruler that wants proof of value before it trusts anything. You give freely to a group, a person or a project once you've committed, and you stay quietly unsure whether the commitment has paid you back.

The Mercury sub-period of your Ketu major period began only weeks ago, and Mercury sits beside your Sun in the first house. This period brings that revising, retrograde habit to the front of how you present yourself. You're re-examining your position within the groups the eleventh house governs, while the Ketu period around it loosens your hold on several of them. What comes forward now is the question of which of those affiliations you go on bending toward and which you're ready to let move past you.
