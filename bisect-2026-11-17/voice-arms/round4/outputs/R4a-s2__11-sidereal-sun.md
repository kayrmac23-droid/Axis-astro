## The Sun

When a situation starts to pin you to one fixed version of yourself, you shift your weight and find another angle on it, and that ease of movement is what keeps you steady. Your Sun sits in Swati, a lunar mansion ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind, and its instinct is to bend with pressure so it doesn't snap, treating movement as a condition of staying well. In the Sidereal frame your Sun moves back one sign, from Scorpio to Libra, and stays in the first house in both frames. So the same body and the same first approach to a room are being described here through a different sign.

### The Sun in Libra, House 1

In Jyotish the Sun in Libra is debilitated, which is the planet at its weakest sign. Here that weakness is in your own person. Libra measures you by how well the exchange in front of you balances. So a clear, self-standing claim to be the one who decides costs you more effort than it costs a Sun placed elsewhere. You can keep finding your position in relation to whoever you're with, and then struggle to say what it was once they've left. Classical technique tempers this, because Mars, lord of the Sun's exaltation sign, sits in an angle counted from your Moon. That condition gives the Sun some backing from outside itself. The debilitation stays, and you still tend to reach your own authority through someone else's terms.

This Sun rules your eleventh house, the house of gains, allies and the goals you set for later, and it carries all of that into the first. Your ambitions and your circle tend to arrive as part of who you are in the body. They aren't a project kept at arm's length. When a hope stalls or a group you belong to cools, you feel it as a change in your own standing. When those goals are moving, you're at your most alive: a steady centre that gives its warmth freely to the people it has chosen to back.

Two companions share the sign with your Sun. Mercury, retrograde, turns the identity back over its own reasoning, so you revisit who you are as often as you revisit what you said. Pluto, a few minutes of arc away, presses the whole first house toward intensity. That same Pluto is why Swati's light, flexible instinct carries weight underneath. You bend, but the bending isn't casual, and a change of position can take something out of you even when it looks effortless from inside the conversation.

No graha casts its gaze on this Sun, so no outside planet sets it off. Its pattern comes and goes with your own circumstances. It's active whenever your body, your presence or your goals are the subject: entering something new, deciding what you want next, being asked who you are in a partnership. The Sun casts its own gaze across to the seventh house, Aries, which holds no planets. So your identity looks constantly toward the other person in an exchange, and finds no fixed counterweight there to push against. The pattern rests when there's nobody to position yourself against and nothing yet to aim for.

The Ketu period running through these years loosens your grip on fixed identity, and the Mercury sub-period that has only just begun brings forward the planet sitting beside your Sun in the first house. What's coming up now is the habit of re-examining yourself: older goals reopened, your sense of who you are tested against your own second thoughts. Ketu's detachment and Mercury's retrograde review are working on the same house at once. So the questions about direction that the eleventh house raises are reaching you as private reconsideration of who you are, and you feel them in the body.
