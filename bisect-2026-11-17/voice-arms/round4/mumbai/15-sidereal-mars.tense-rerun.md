## Mars

### Mars in Gemini, House 7

When you push for something, the push lands in three places at once: your work and standing, your own body and plans, and your money. Mars sends its gaze, which is what drishti means, from the seventh house to three others. It reaches the tenth, where Uranus and Pluto sit. It reaches Jupiter in your Lagna. And it reaches Rahu in the second. So a fight with a partner rarely stays between the two of you. It changes how you show up at work the next day, it sets your health and direction moving, and it often ends in a decision about spending or earning that you made while still heated.

Mars keeps its Gemini sign and seventh house in both frames, so the talking, provoking style of drive you already know carries over. What this frame adds is where the consequences travel.

The gaze onto the tenth is the sharpest of the three, because Uranus and Pluto there are already charged. A disagreement at home can turn into an abrupt move at work, such as a resignation drafted or a superior challenged. The suddenness comes from the relationship, not from anything in the job itself. The gaze onto Rahu feeds the second-house hunger. After a clash you go after money or security with extra appetite, as if winning the argument had to be banked somewhere.

Mars rules your fifth and twelfth houses, and it carries both into partnership. Your creative appetite and romantic daring get spent on one person, and so do your losses, your expenses and the things you'd prefer not to say. You give a partner your boldest, most playful self, and you pay for the relationship in sleep, money and privacy.

Venus sits beside Mars in Mrigashira, the searching nakshatra you met in the Venus section, and it keeps desire and curiosity fused. Jupiter's gaze from the Lagna is what sends the force back to you. When a partner's push meets your own conviction, you slow down and take the broad view. You then often defend someone you love with surprising stamina, going to the wall once you believe the fight is worth having. Without that conviction, the same push scatters into argument for its own sake.

Gemini is an enemy's sign for Mars in Jyotish, because Mercury, its ruler, is a natural adversary of Mars. Whenever your drive is called on, it has to work through a medium that keeps questioning it. You want to act, and the sign hands you another angle to consider and another sentence to try first. Courage arrives as negotiation, and a plain demand gets dressed as a joke. Mercury itself sits in Aries in your fifth and lends some heat back, but the friction stays. You start bravely while the outcome is still uncertain, then spend energy defending your right to have started, and that leaves less for finishing what you began.
