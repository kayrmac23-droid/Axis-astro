## The Moon

### The Moon in Pisces, House 4

Whether you feel safe depends almost entirely on whether your sense of direction is intact, because in this frame the Moon sits in a sign that neither shelters it nor works against it. Pisces is neutral ground for the Moon in Jyotish, so the sign hands your feelings no automatic comfort and no built-in strain. The weight falls on its ruler, Jupiter, which is strong in its own sign in your Lagna. The Moon stays in Pisces in the fourth house in both frames, and the private room it needs is already familiar to you. What this frame adds is a condition for that room. When you're moving toward something you believe in, home settles around you and your moods come and go without alarm. When your bearings slip, the house can be perfectly calm and you still can't rest in it. Because Jupiter is retrograde, you rarely trace the unease back to its source, and you tend to blame the room for a loss of direction.

The Moon also rules your eighth house, so crises, shared money and what can't be spoken about openly all end up at your own front door. Other people's emergencies and the hidden business of a family get worked through at home, in your own feelings, long before they're settled anywhere else. You often know the secret, or carry the strain of a joint obligation, inside the one place meant to be free of it.

Uranus and Pluto both cast their gaze onto your Moon from Virgo in the tenth, and they set the pattern moving. News from work, a sudden reorganisation or a superior's exacting judgement doesn't stay at work. You bring it through the door, and the mood of the whole evening can flip in a moment, with Uranus supplying the abruptness and Pluto the sense that something has to be controlled. Your Moon looks straight back at the tenth, so your feelings keep a constant watch on your standing. You tend to read a good week at home and a good week in your career as one thing, and a setback in one leaves you unsure of the other. What this costs you is a home that belongs to you alone, without your working life drifting through it.

Saturn's sub-period, running until early 2027 inside the Venus chapter, matters to this Moon because Saturn rules its nakshatra. The demand for patience and endurance falls directly on your emotional life right now, and you're probably holding more than you show.

That holding is the instinct of Uttara Bhadrapada, the nakshatra your Moon occupies, ruled by Saturn and presided over by Ahir Budhnya, the serpent of the deep. It gives your feelings the quality of deep water: still on the surface, with a great deal contained below. The pattern surfaces when something heavy lands and you can't yet put words to it. In that moment your instinct is to sink it, contain it and wait until it has settled into something you understand. Often that patience gives you a calm judgement that others can lean on during a crisis. Under strain it means a feeling can sit unspoken in you for a season, and the people living with you only feel the stillness, without ever learning what it holds.
