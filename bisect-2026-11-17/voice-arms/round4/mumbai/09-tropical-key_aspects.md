## Key Aspects

### Neptune Sextile Pluto

You let a belief carry you into a role and then let the role remake the belief, often without noticing the trade. Neptune sits in Sagittarius in your first house, so faith is part of how you enter things. Pluto sits in Virgo in your tenth house, a few degrees short of your Midheaven, so the force that changes you comes through work and public standing. The sextile between them is moderate and applying, and it gives the two a working channel. When a job or a public responsibility strips away something you were sure of, you tend to rebuild the conviction around what the exacting experience taught you, and you come out believing in something more precise.

Its weight is lighter than the tight squares that run your relationships. At 4.7° it's a supporting current, and both planets move slowly, so you share this contact with everyone born around the same years. What makes it yours is where it lands. It links your rising-sign faith to the most charged point of your public life. Pluto there also squares your Mars and Venus and opposes your Moon, so the private pressure arrives through the same doorway.

What it takes from you is the sense of having chosen your beliefs. Retrograde on both ends, the exchange happens privately. You notice afterwards that a view you hold firmly arrived through scrutiny, a demanding standard or a role that reshaped you, and the faith feels like your own discovery.

Your worldview gets updated by your work more than by your reflection. A hard stretch of being measured and corrected in public goes into your private sense of what life is for, and you tend to come away steadier and better informed for it. The cost is that you find it hard to say what you believed before, or whether the person who walked into that stretch would recognise the one who walked out.
