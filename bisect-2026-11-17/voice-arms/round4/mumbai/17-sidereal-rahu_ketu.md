## Rahu and Ketu

### Rahu in Capricorn, House 2

Your appetite for earned worth runs on a long clock in this frame, because Rahu sits in Uttara Ashadha, a nakshatra ruled by the Sun and presided over by the Vishvadevas, the gods of universal principle. The node keeps its sign and house in both charts, so the hunger to stand on resources you built yourself is already familiar. What Uttara Ashadha adds is patience with a principle behind it: you can defer a reward for years if the thing you're building feels right as well as profitable, and you want your standing to hold up after you've stopped pushing for it. Under strain that patience stiffens, so you keep postponing enjoyment on the grounds that the foundation isn't finished.

Mars looks onto this Rahu from Gemini in your seventh, and that's what sets the hunger going. An argument with a partner, a rival's quick boast, or a deal talked through with one other person tends to leave you wanting to prove your worth in figures. You come away from the exchange and start reworking a budget or chasing a raise, and the ambition then carries the heat of the quarrel more than your own priorities. Saturn, which rules Capricorn, sits in Taurus in your sixth, so whatever you're reaching for gets paid for through daily labour, hour by hour, at the speed your stamina allows.

### Ketu in Cancer, House 8

Ketu also holds its place in both frames, and here it sits in Pushya, ruled by Saturn and presided over by Brihaspati, the divine teacher. Pushya's instinct is to nourish steadily, the way a good teacher keeps feeding a student long after the novelty wears off. In the eighth house that care goes into other people's crises, debts and inheritances, and you sustain someone through a hard season without counting the months. Saturn looks onto Ketu from your sixth, so other people's emergencies get absorbed into your working routine as duties, and you can carry one for a year while feeling oddly absent from it.

Saturn connects both ends of this axis, ruling Rahu's sign and Ketu's nakshatra, and Saturn's sub-period within the Venus chapter runs to February 2027. Your working days are where the question gets settled. Each week offers you two kinds of steady effort: the slow building of something that's yours, and the dependable holding of someone else's trouble. The second comes so naturally that it fills the hours first. What you lose is the evidence, in your own accounts, that the patience Uttara Ashadha gives you was ever spent on you.
