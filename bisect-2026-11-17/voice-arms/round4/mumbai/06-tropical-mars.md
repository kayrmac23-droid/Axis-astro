## Mars

You want to settle a disagreement the moment it starts, in words, out loud, while it's still hot, and you also want it never to have happened at all, so a quarrel with someone close tends to get half-fought and then carried home unfinished. Mars in Gemini in the seventh house is the half that wants to say it now; the Moon in Pisces in the fourth, squaring it tightly, is the half that would rather let it melt into the walls. From Mars's side, the consequence is that your fights rarely conclude. The impulse fires, the Moon refuses to let the bond be risked, and because that Moon doesn't release what it has merged with, an argument you dropped mid-sentence can stay alive in you for weeks, replayed in private with all the good lines you didn't use.

### Mars in Gemini, House 7

When something matters to you, you go after it by talking. You ask questions, test the other person's position from three directions, keep several options open and argue for whichever one is winning that minute. Gemini makes your drive quick and changeable, and the seventh house points it at one person at a time: you assert yourself most freely in a direct exchange, with someone who answers back, and least freely on your own. Mars is peregrine here and borrows its character from its ruler, Mercury, which is also peregrine and sits in Aries in your fifth. That's why your force arrives as wit and provocation, more often than as a plain demand. You'll needle, joke and debate toward what you want, and it can take you a long time to state the want itself.

Frustration shows up first as restlessness: you talk faster, switch subjects, can't sit still with a decision. Then, because the Moon won't let the heat land on someone you love, it goes quiet in you and comes out later as fatigue. Gemini's courage is real. You'll start a hard conversation before you know how it ends, and you'll keep finding new ways back into it. Under strain the same flexibility splits you, and you end up arguing both sides until you no longer know which one you held.

### Aspects

The square to Pluto in Virgo in your tenth house is applying and tight, and it's what turns your quick assertion into a contest over control. When someone with authority over you, or someone who holds the standards you're judged by, pushes back, you don't fold; you get precise and relentless, finding the flaw in their reasoning and pressing it. That can win you real ground, because Pluto lends your drive a stamina Gemini doesn't have on its own. It also means a disagreement about a detail becomes a question of who gets to decide, and once it's that, you find it very hard to let the other person keep any point at all. What it takes from you is the ordinary, low-stakes argument: with this square, even small friction with someone who has power over you tends to feel like it's about survival.

The conjunction with Venus means your assertion usually arrives charming, so people often take your real objection as banter and miss that you meant it. The sextile to Mercury gives you the habit of talking yourself into action: you rarely know what you'll do until you've said it aloud to someone.

Once something genuinely matters, you open with a question that's really a challenge, and you keep the exchange moving until the other person has to commit to a position you can push against. You push well. If they're a partner, the heat feels close to affection and you often get what you came for, though the Moon's pull usually stops you from naming the want straight out. If they hold authority, you tend to win the argument and lose some of the goodwill, and you carry the episode home, where your Pisces Moon softens it into tiredness instead of letting it finish. What you end up with is usually the thing you pursued, plus an unfinished conversation still running privately in your head.
