## Mercury

### Mercury in Aries, House 5

Your thinking about partnership and career gets done in the arena of play, so the decisions that shape your marriage and your working standing tend to be made while you're inventing, flirting or riffing on an idea. Mercury rules your seventh and tenth houses from the Lagna and sits in the fifth, which carries both of those serious arenas into the house of pleasure and creation. Mercury keeps the same sign and house in both frames, so the playful, quick-starting mind you've already met stays put. What this frame adds is that your commitments and your vocation answer to it. A career move often begins as a project you took up for fun, and an agreement with a partner often starts as a joke that turned out to be binding.

Aries is neutral ground for Mercury in Jyotish, neither a sign that supports it nor one that works against it. That leaves the nakshatra to set the instinct. Mercury sits in Ashwini, ruled by Ketu and presided over by the Ashwini Kumaras, the divine physicians, and its pull is toward the swift fix. When something is broken, whether a plan, a misunderstanding or a friend's bad week, you move to mend it before you've fully understood what went wrong, and the speed often works because your first read is accurate. It costs you when the problem needed diagnosing more than treating. You patch the symptom, call the matter settled, and the same trouble comes back in a form you didn't see coming, because the eagerness got there ahead of the attention.

Mercury shares Aries with your exalted Sun, which completes the Raja Yoga linking your fortune with your partnerships and career. In practice your opinions and your sense of purpose arrive together, so it's hard for you to hold a thought lightly once it's yours. A disagreement about an idea tends to feel like a challenge to your direction.

Jupiter's gaze from your Lagna is what tests this mind and steadies it. When a teacher, a book or your own larger conviction backs an idea, you think it through more slowly and see it to the end, and the Ashwini rush gets a frame that makes your quick fixes into lasting ones. Jupiter is retrograde, so that backing usually comes from inside you, as a private sense that the idea is worth the trouble. Mercury's own gaze falls on an empty eleventh, so groups, networks and wider circles rarely sharpen your thinking or check it. Your ideas get tested in one conversation, against one person, or against your own beliefs, and an idea that has never met a room of people can go a long way before anyone catches what it missed.
