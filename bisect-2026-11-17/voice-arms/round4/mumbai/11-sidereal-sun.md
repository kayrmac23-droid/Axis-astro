## The Sun

### The Sun in Aries, House 5

When you make something, you're willing to see it through its ending as well as its beginning: you'll start a piece of work, an affair or a plan with full heat, and you can also be the one who calls it finished and takes responsibility for what that costs. That capacity comes from Bharani, the nakshatra your Sun occupies, ruled by Venus and presided over by Yama, the figure of death and accountable judgement, so your creative fire carries a sense that whatever you bring into being, you answer for. In this frame the Sun moves back one sign, from Taurus in the sixth to Aries in the fifth, and the self described here is the one that shows up when you're creating, playing or falling for someone. That's a different situation from the daily round, where your sense of yourself waits on another person's reading of you.

Here the Sun is exalted, which in Jyotish means a planet in the sign where it functions at full strength, and in Aries that strength is the nerve to go first. In the fifth house it goes into whatever you make for its own sake: a project begun on an impulse, a romance you pursue openly, a performance or a game you throw yourself into. In that arena you don't need anyone to sign off on who you are, and you act from a steady centre that needs no audience to confirm it. Exaltation also overshoots. Once you're lit, you can push a creative idea or a courtship harder than it can bear, and the Bharani weight means you then feel personally answerable for the wreckage.

The Sun rules your ninth house, so belief, teachers and the sense of what your life is for travel into the fifth with it. Your faith tends to get proved through what you make. When a piece of work comes off, you feel your direction confirmed; when it fails, the doubt reaches past the work into whether you've understood your life at all. Mercury shares the sign, which is part of the Raja Yoga already described, and it means you tend to find out what you believe by saying it while you're in the middle of making something.

Jupiter, strong in its own sign in your Lagna, casts its gaze onto this Sun, and that's what sets it going. A mentor's encouragement, a book that opens a door, or simply your own conviction that something is worth trying is usually what gets you started, and your boldness grows the moment you feel backed by a larger meaning. Without that backing the drive stalls oddly. Your Sun's own gaze falls on an empty eleventh, so wider circles and groups rarely supply the push.

Venus mahadasha runs until 2031, and Venus is Bharani's lord, so this whole chapter keeps pleasure, attraction and creative appetite close to the centre of your sense of self. Within it, the Saturn sub-period runs until early 2027, and Saturn, ruling your second and third houses from the sixth, presses money, effort and routine obligations against the fifth-house impulse. Right now you're probably finding that whatever you want to make has to be paid for in hours and earnings first, and the Bharani part of you is weighing which pleasures you can stand behind and which ones it's time to end.
