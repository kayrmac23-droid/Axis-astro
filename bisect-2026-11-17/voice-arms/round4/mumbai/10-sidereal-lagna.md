## The Lagna — Ascendant in Jyotish

### Sagittarius Lagna

Your life tends to arrange itself around a teacher, a belief or a direction you've chosen, and when one is in place your body and circumstances seem to cooperate with you. Sagittarius rises in this frame too, at its first degree, so the fire-and-mutable lean toward the point of things carries over unchanged. What differs is the ruler. In the Tropical chart Jupiter sits in fall in the second and audits your generosity after the fact. Here Jupiter is in its own sign and sits in the Lagna itself, which describes something else: a constitution and a run of circumstances that hold up while you're moving toward something you believe in. The Tropical account is about what you count afterwards. This one is about the ground under your feet while you're still walking.

Jupiter in its own sign in the first house forms Hamsa Yoga, one of the five classical combinations in which a planet strong in its own sign occupies an angle, and it's the clearest signal in this chart. It tends to give you a sturdy, recoverable body, a measure of protection in hard stretches, and a habit of finding the mentor, the book or the opening at about the moment you need it. Jupiter also rules your fourth house, so home and inner steadiness are tied to the same faith. Because Jupiter is retrograde, that assurance mostly stays private. You tend to trust your own judgement quietly and then feel uncertain when it's asked to explain itself out loud. When you're asked to perform conviction on demand, the support goes thin.

Venus and Mars cast their gaze onto your Lagna from Gemini in the seventh, and that's what sets this circumstance moving. You tend to change direction because of a partner, a rival or a quick, persuasive person across from you, since their arguments and attractions land directly on your body and your plans. Venus rules your sixth and Mars your twelfth, so both bring the difficult houses of work strain, illness and loss into the house of partnership. You often take on extra labour, expense or exhaustion through the people you're closest to, and your health is usually the first thing to register a relationship running hot.

The wider shape of your life leans on what you make. The lord of your ninth, the Sun, is exalted in Aries in the fifth, sitting with Mercury, which rules your seventh and tenth. That pairing forms a Raja Yoga, an alignment that links your fortune and your partnerships with your career. Your standing tends to come through creative work, teaching, speculation or anything you build from play, so your vocation is usually won by making something more than by climbing a ladder. The Moon, ruling your eighth, sits in the fourth, so upheavals and inheritances usually pass through your home before anywhere else.

You're in Venus mahadasha, with the Saturn sub-period running until early 2027. Saturn rules your second and third houses from the sixth, which brings the present weight down onto money, effort and daily obligation, while Venus keeps partnership at the centre of the larger chapter. Right now you're probably carrying a lot of work through a relationship, and you're steady enough underneath to manage it. What it takes from you is easy rest, because your body keeps paying the costs of agreements made in someone else's company while your faith tells you the arrangement is worth it.
