## The Lunar Nodes

### Rahu in Capricorn, House 2

You want to be worth something you built yourself, and the wanting has a hunger to it that ordinary ambition lacks. Rahu in Capricorn in the second house points your growth toward earned, measurable standing: savings you accumulated, skill you can name a price for, a footing nobody handed you. Because you're reaching past what you were given, it tends to feel awkward at first, so you overwork the plan or set a target nobody asked for. Saturn rules this Rahu from your seventh house, which means even this solitary appetite gets its terms from partnership, and a promise to someone close quietly decides how far you let yourself climb.

The tight trine to Pluto in the tenth is where the hunger gets its nerve. When public scrutiny or a demanding authority strips something away, you tend to rebuild your resources harder and smarter than before, turning a loss of standing into a reason to secure more of it. The Moon's sextile means that when this works, it calms you physically, and you can feel safer after balancing a budget than after any reassurance. The Mercury square keeps the fixation running in your head. The moderate sextile to Neptune lends the whole project a private faith that the effort leads somewhere.

### Ketu in Cancer, House 8

Your practised groove is tending other people's depths. Ketu in Cancer in the eighth makes you fluent at holding someone through a crisis, sharing their fears and their money troubles, sinking into what's merged between you, and you do it without effort or credit. It also lets you see when an entanglement has stopped being yours to carry. Leaning on it lets you look after someone else's security in place of building your own, and under strain you go through the motions of closeness while feeling oddly absent from them.

You're most at home inside someone else's emergency and most uneasy when the only thing left to secure is your own worth. So you tend to answer a friend's crisis at once and leave your own ledger for later, and that delay costs you the plain experience of building something solely because you wanted it.
