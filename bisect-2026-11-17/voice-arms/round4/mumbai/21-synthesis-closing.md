## Living the Divergence

What this person has built, over time, is a habit of sorting. The work they repeat goes in one column and stays provisional, waiting for someone close to say whether it was any good. The work they invent goes in another and is owned from the first stroke. The Taurus Sun in the sixth and the exalted Aries Sun in the fifth each get their own stretch of the week, and the two stretches rarely meet. Generosity gets the same treatment. The Sidereal Jupiter in its own sign carries the start of a venture, and the Tropical Jupiter in fall keeps the books on it afterwards, so they seldom have to feel the renewal and the debit in the same hour.

The arrangement has a seam, and it runs through their working life. When their output is assessed, the Libra Midheaven asks whether everyone involved is still on good terms, and the Virgo Midheaven asks whether the thing is exact. Their sorting has no column for a verdict that needs both. Saturn's close opposition to Neptune is what reopens the seam. When a shared task starts to blur, one account sends them to tidy someone else's loose ends, and the other sends them back into their own hours to cover a shortfall they can't trace. They usually do both, one after the other, without deciding which kind of blur it was. With Saturn's sub-period inside the Venus chapter lasting to February 2027, that double response is a regular feature of their weeks.

What the system takes from them is time spent filing. Each event in their day gets assigned to one chart or the other before they let themselves react to it, and the assigning is its own small labour, done many times a day. Most days the sorting keeps the two accounts from colliding. It doesn't tell them which column they're standing in when someone asks them, plainly, how they're doing.
