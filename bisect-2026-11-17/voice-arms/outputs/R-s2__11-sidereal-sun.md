## The Sun

Your Sun moves back one sign in the Sidereal frame, from Scorpio to Libra at 15°51', and stays in the first house in both systems. When it is active, you keep yourself movable. You leave a room, a plan or a position the moment it begins to fix you in place, and you return when you choose. That instinct comes from Swati, the nakshatra this Sun occupies, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind. Libra alone would explain the weighing. Swati explains the bending: you give way under pressure so that you are not pinned, and you count your freedom by whether you can still leave.

### Where the self has to work

In Jyotish the Sun is debilitated in Libra, and the first house puts that difficulty in your body and your daily presence. You make identity through relationship, through the fair reading and the balanced reply, and that is a sign that resists a planet whose work is to stand alone and be counted. So the steady centre the Sun is meant to give you comes harder. You often find your own view only after hearing everyone else's, and you hold it less firmly once you have found it. Nothing else in this placement makes up for that.

One classical condition softens it: Mars, lord of Aries where the Sun is exalted, sits in a kendra from your Moon. Sources disagree on how much this cancels. It is fair to read it as a partial easing, and nothing more.

The Sun rules your eleventh house, Leo, and brings it into the first. What you hope for, and the circles you belong to, get carried in your own person. Your standing among friends and allies depends on how you conduct yourself in the room, more than on any network you have built. The Sun's gaze falls on your seventh house, and that house is empty. You look toward partnership and find no planet waiting there to answer, so whatever balance you are after has to be made fresh with each person.

### What shares the house

Mercury and Pluto sit in Libra with the Sun. Mercury keeps your identity talking to itself, testing the fair version of who you are. Pluto pushes that testing below the surface, so a small adjustment in how you present yourself can feel like a much larger question about who you are.

This pattern is live whenever a relationship asks you to state where you stand: an agreement being set, a group deciding, a friend wanting your verdict. It goes quiet when nobody needs a position from you. In those stretches the Libra Sun's warmth runs freely toward whatever you have chosen, and the Swati habit of moving loses its urgency.

The Ketu mahadasha runs until October 2027, and its Mercury sub-period has only just begun. Because Mercury shares the Sun's sign and house, this year brings your self-definition into the foreground of a period otherwise organised around releasing things. Ketu loosens your hold on old positions, and Mercury asks you to put a new one into words. Mercury is retrograde, so that wording tends to come back for revision before it settles. The debilitated Sun is asked to name itself under the same conditions that make naming hard.
