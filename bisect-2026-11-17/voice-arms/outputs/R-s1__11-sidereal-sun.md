## The Sun

Your Sun moves back one sign in this frame, from Scorpio to Libra at 15°51', and stays in the first house in both. Here it sits in Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind, and when that nakshatra is active you bend toward whatever pressure is in the room, give ground, take a different line through it, and come out the far side still moving. Libra alone would describe the wish for balance. Swati adds the need to keep shifting position, so that staying put in one stance for too long registers in you as a kind of suffocation.

### Living with a debilitated centre

In Jyotish this Sun is debilitated: the planet of will and self-authority placed in the sign where it has least purchase. In the first house that weakness belongs to the body and the circumstances you move through, so asserting a plain "this is what I want" costs you more effort than the result seems to justify, and often you settle for the version everyone can live with. That cost stays a cost. A steadier capacity sits beside it. When you have committed to someone or something, you give warmth to it without needing to perform it, and Swati's flexibility lets that warmth survive changes of plan that would break a more rigid loyalty.

One classical condition softens the debilitation. Mars, lord of Aries where the Sun is exalted, sits in a kendra from your Moon. Sources weigh this differently. What it describes is a will that recovers some force through the Mars side of the chart, in the twelfth house, out of sight, and not through the Sun's own position.

The Sun rules the eleventh house, gains, networks and the circles you belong to, and carries that house into the first. Your allies, your hopes and what comes back to you from groups get worn on the person. You tend to measure your own standing by whether the circle around you is in balance, and when it is not, the imbalance feels like a fault in you.

### The planets sharing the sign

Mercury and Pluto share Libra with the Sun. Pluto, a degree away, means the bending is never light. Even a concession carries weight, and giving ground can feel like a small death of position. Mercury, retrograde, means the reasons for any stance you take get reworked again after you have voiced it. The Sun casts its drishti on an empty seventh house, Aries, so your attention goes outward toward a partner, an opponent or an equal, and finds no planet there to answer it. This pattern is live wherever you are paired or measured against someone. It goes quiet in work you carry alone, where nobody needs to be balanced against you.

You are in the Ketu mahadasha, and the Mercury antardasha has only just begun. Ketu loosens attachment to outcomes, which leaves a debilitated Sun even less inclined to insist. Mercury, conjunct this Sun in the first house, now brings retrograde re-examination to the front of daily life. Expect the coming year to centre on how you explain yourself to the people beside you. Positions you conceded earlier come back for review, and you feel the strain between Vayu's urge to keep moving and Mercury's urge to go back over the ground.
