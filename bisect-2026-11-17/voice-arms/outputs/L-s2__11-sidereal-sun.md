## The Sun

The Sun moves back one sign between the frames, from Tropical Scorpio to Sidereal Libra, and stays in the first house in both. Placed in Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind god, it gives you an instinct to keep moving whenever a position starts to set around you. You shift your stance, change the subject, take the more flexible reading, and you feel steadier for having done it. Swati bends so that it does not break, and its need for freedom of movement is something Libra's sign description alone would not show you.

### The Sun in Libra, Debilitated

In Jyotish the Sun is debilitated in Libra. The planet of self-assertion has to work through a sign that measures every move against how it lands with someone else, so your will tends to be shaped by the terms of the exchange before you have stated it. Being recognised for a position you held on your own, unadjusted, is the specific thing that costs you most effort here. It stays costly, and nothing elsewhere in this frame pays it back.

One classical cancellation condition is met. Mars, lord of Aries, where the Sun is exalted, sits in a kendra from the Moon, in Virgo in the twelfth. Sources weigh this differently. It names a support that runs out of sight, and the debilitation still stands beside it.

### Lordship and Companions

The Sun rules the eleventh house, the house of gains, networks and long-range hopes, and carries it into the first. What you hope to gain and the circles you gain it through become matters of your own body and bearing. Your ambitions are something you wear, and you meet them each morning in the way you present yourself to whoever is in front of you. Under strain that becomes a fixation on whether your standing in a group is holding.

Mercury, retrograde, and Pluto share the sign. Mercury turns the self over again before it is shown. Pluto, nearly at the Sun's degree, presses intensity into a placement built for diplomacy. The Sun casts its seventh-house drishti into an empty Aries. No graha receives it, so your will has nobody stationed in the house of partnership to answer it.

The arena is the first house: your bearing, your health, the stance you take on arrival. The pattern is live when a group expects you to state yourself and the terms keep moving under you. It goes quiet when you are moving: travelling, between roles, free to change course without anyone keeping score. In those conditions the Swati flexibility stops costing you anything, and the constructive side of this Sun shows. You become a steady centre that bends without losing its shape, giving warmth to what you have chosen to commit to.

The Ketu mahadasha, running until 2027, already loosens your hold on outward recognition. The Mercury antardasha has only just begun, and it hands the timing to the planet sitting in this Sun's own sign. For roughly the coming year, the private revision that Mercury performs on the self moves into the foreground of a period already given to release. The questions this Sun's eleventh-house lordship raises, about which aims and which networks are still yours, are being reopened now. They will be settled by what you keep after you stop adjusting.
