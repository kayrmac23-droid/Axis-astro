## Where They Part

The Tropical chart assembles this person around Scorpio and a Mars in detriment. The Sidereal chart assembles them around Libra and a Venus in detriment. Every divergence below follows from that exchange of rulers, and each one plays out in a particular kind of situation.

### Mars: Libra in Detriment, Virgo Peregrine

The Tropical Mars sits at Libra 15°56' in the twelfth house, in detriment, and it rules the Scorpio Ascendant. Its own ruler is Venus in Scorpio, also in detriment. Neither link in that chain holds its own ground, so the will that leads the whole Tropical chart decides privately and defers, and its dispositor gives it no clean support. The Sidereal Mars sits at Virgo 22°15', still in the twelfth, peregrine, in Hasta Pada 4. Its ruler is Mercury in Libra in the first house, also peregrine and retrograde. Here Mars rules the second and seventh houses and does not lead the chart. Its condition is plain, neither dignified nor compromised, and the twelfth-house labour takes the form of correcting things by hand.

The close sextile to Saturn, applying at 2.8°, sets off the scene. A commitment already made turns out to need defending: an agreed figure, a standing arrangement around what they hold. In the Tropical account, they weigh whether pressing the point would upset the balance of the agreement, then bring it forward as a proposal. That keeps the arrangement intact and leaves the want unspoken. In the Sidereal account, they go back over the terms alone and refine them until nothing in them can be faulted. What they hold afterwards is a flawless version that nobody watched being made. By dignity and dispositor, the Tropical chain is under heavier pressure. The Sidereal chain is under less, and it has its own cost: the correction never reaches the room.

### The Ascendant: Scorpio 6°57', Libra 13°16'

The Tropical rising sign is fixed water. It enters a situation by testing for what is concealed, and its ruler is that compromised Mars. The Sidereal Lagna is cardinal air. It enters by measuring whether the exchange has settled into balance, and its lord is Venus, in detriment in the second house. In the Sidereal frame, Venus conjoins Saturn there in a Raja Yoga. The two thresholds meet whenever this person walks into a negotiation. One instinct reads it for who holds hidden power, and the other reads it for whether the terms are fair. The two readings do not combine. Within the same minute they can come away suspicious of the arrangement and also obliged to keep it even. Each frame's ruler sits in detriment, so neither threshold rests on a dignified lord.

### The Sun: Scorpio Peregrine, Libra in Fall

The Tropical Sun sits at Scorpio 9°32' in the first house, peregrine, and Mars in detriment disposes it. Identity here works as depth, and the self is defined by what it will not leave unexamined. The Sidereal Sun sits at Libra 15°51' in the first house, debilitated, in Swati Pada 3. Venus in detriment disposes it. Classically the debilitation is cancelled because Mars stands angular from the Moon. Even so, laying a plain claim to selfhood here means first checking one's own standing against everyone else's.

The tight conjunction to Pluto, 0.4° and separating, sets off the scene. Someone makes a careless remark about where this person stands. In the Tropical account, they register it as a breach and go straight to the motive underneath it. They come away clear about what happened, with the exchange heavier than anyone else had made it. In the Sidereal account, Pluto shares the Libra placement, so the same remark sends them reweighing their own position against the speaker's. They come away with the verdict still pending. The Sidereal Sun carries more pressure by dignity. The close Neptune sextile, at 3.7° and separating, runs through both accounts as an available capacity to read the other person's feeling, and neither frame lets it decide the outcome.

### Jupiter: Aries Peregrine, Pisces Domicile

The Tropical Jupiter sits at Aries 22°43' in the sixth house, peregrine and retrograde, with Mars in detriment as its dispositor. The urge to improve work and the body is direct, and the will it depends on to act stalls in the twelfth house. The Sidereal Jupiter sits at Pisces 29°02', in its own sign in the sixth house, in Revati Pada 4, and forms Harsha Yoga. It disposes itself, so the same arena takes in the whole of a routine's condition with nothing in the way. The Sidereal position falls within the last degree of Pisces and the Tropical one is clear of any sign boundary. That is a difference between the frames and nothing more.

The tight trine to Uranus, 1.6° and separating, sets off the scene. A routine breaks, and starting again on different terms becomes possible. In the Tropical account, they decide on a fresh regimen at once, and acting on it waits on a Mars that defers. They are left holding a plan that is ready and not yet begun. In the Sidereal account, they take on the disruption and absorb the extra work as part of the job. They are left holding more than they had before, competently handled, with no clear point at which the load stopped growing. The Tropical Jupiter carries more pressure because its dispositor is compromised. The Sidereal Jupiter carries an absence of internal resistance, and its cost is how much it accumulates.

The divergence stays open across Ketu, the Midheaven, Rahu, the Moon, Neptune, Mercury, Pluto, Saturn, Venus and Uranus, and each widens the distance between the two accounts without being settled here.

Across all four divergences, this person inhabits two chains of rule. In one, Mars in detriment runs the identity, the threshold and the work. In the other, Venus in detriment runs identity and threshold while Jupiter rules its own arena. The same twelfth-house Mars either leads the whole chart or tends two houses from the margin, depending on the frame. It is easy for them to mistake the deferral for a personal failing, when in one account it is the governing structure and in the other it is a local habit. Both accounts describe the same week of their life, and each is accurate on its own evidence.
