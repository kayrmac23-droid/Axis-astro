## Where They Part

Mars carries the widest split in this chart, because in the Tropical frame it rules the Ascendant and in the Sidereal frame it rules neither the Lagna nor the Sun's sign. The Tropical Mars sits at 15°56' Libra in the twelfth house, in detriment, and its dispositor is Venus in Scorpio in the first, also in detriment. The pattern is a will that has to negotiate with a sign built for agreement, and then report to a ruler that trusts nothing it has not tested. The Sidereal Mars sits at 22°15' Virgo, still in the twelfth, peregrine, in Hasta, and its dispositor is Mercury in Libra in the first house, peregrine and retrograde. Read with Jyotish technique, this is a will that works by correction, answering to a mind that revises its own position in the body and the persona.

The sextile to Saturn, close at 2.8° and applying, is where the two accounts lead this person to act differently. In both frames Saturn sits in the second house, so the trigger is the same: something owed to them inside a commitment already made, a fee, a share, a term of an agreement that has slipped. Under the Tropical account, they weigh what would be fair to both sides and arrive with a proposal pitched where the other person can accept it. What they hold afterwards is a settlement lighter than the want that started it, reached cleanly and resented quietly. Under the Sidereal account, they draft the case and correct it until no clause is weak, and Mercury's retrograde habit sends the draft back for another pass. What they hold afterwards is a precise, defensible argument that arrives late, sometimes after the moment it was meant for has closed. The wide opposition to Jupiter adds a thin tilt toward expecting the other party to overreach.

The Ascendant parts along the element line. The Tropical rising sign is Scorpio at 6°57', fixed water, and it makes the threshold of every situation an act of assessment: who is concealing what, and where the current runs. The Sidereal Lagna is Libra at 13°16', cardinal air, and it makes the same threshold an act of arrangement: whether the terms between people have settled, and who has given what. The Sun-Pluto conjunction, tight at 0.4°, sits on this angle in both frames, so whenever someone new enters this person's orbit the intensity arrives first. Under Scorpio, they read the newcomer for motive and withhold judgement until it has been earned. Under Libra, they start setting the terms of the exchange, offering and measuring. The first leaves them with a suspicion they cannot yet justify. The second leaves them with an agreement they half-believe, because the Lagna's lord is Venus in detriment and the settlement is only as trusted as that Venus allows.

The Sun follows the Ascendant across the line, and its dignity changes with it. The Tropical Sun at 9°32' Scorpio in the first house is peregrine, running at its own pressure, disposited by the Libra Mars in detriment. Identity here is depth, and its difficulty is delivery, since the ruler holds back what the Sun would press forward. The Sidereal Sun at 15°51' Libra in the first is in fall, in Swati, disposited by Venus in Scorpio in detriment in the second. Identity here is calibration, and its difficulty sits at the root: claiming plain selfhood costs effort before anything else happens, and the ruler that might license it is itself compromised.

The sextile to Neptune, close at 3.7°, sets the moment off: an intuitive read of what someone else needs, arriving unbidden in a conversation where this person's own wish is also on the table. Under the Tropical account, they use the read to go underneath, naming what the other person has not said, and come away having steered the exchange while their own wish stayed unstated. Under the Sidereal account, they let the other person's need set the balance and place their own claim after it, and come away with the room settled and their standing in it unasked for. The two Suns cost different things. One spends the self on depth it cannot always deliver. The other defers the self until a verdict arrives that the fall makes slow to come.

Jupiter shifts from strain to ease in the sixth house. The Tropical Jupiter at 22°43' Aries, peregrine and retrograde, is disposited by the detriment Mars in the twelfth, so the urge to start the improved routine depends on a will that weighs in private first. The Sidereal Jupiter at 29°02' Pisces is in its own sign in the last degree of it, retrograde, in Revati, disposited by itself, and it carries Harsha Yoga: a difficulty-house lord confined to its own house, where adversity in work and health classically turns to advantage, though never to ease.

The trine to Uranus, tight at 1.6°, is the trigger: a disruption in the work routine or the body's regimen that opens a door. Under the Tropical account, they launch the overhaul with Aries conviction and then stall while the dispositor deliberates, holding a half-built new system beside the old one. Under the Sidereal account, they absorb the disruption and expand into it without friction, taking on the larger load because nothing in a domicile Jupiter registers a limit. What that leaves them holding is more work and no marked edge where it should have stopped.

The divergence continues, unwalked here, across Ketu, the Midheaven, Rahu, the Moon, Neptune, Mercury, Pluto, Saturn, Venus and Uranus, each drawn differently by the two accounts and none of them settled.

The Ketu mahadasha with its Mercury antardasha, both running to 2027, puts the Sidereal dispositor of this Mars in the foreground of the present chapter. Between the frames, the open question this person lives with concerns what the delay in their action consists of. The Tropical account reads it as a will forced through a sign of agreement and a ruler of suspicion; the Sidereal account reads it as a will routed through correction and a mind that sends its own work back. Both describe the same late arrival. They disagree about what the time was spent on, so this person can never be sure, when the demand finally reaches the other side of the table, whether they softened it or perfected it, and the two answers ask different things of them.
