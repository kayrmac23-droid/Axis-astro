## The Sun

The Sun moves back one sign between the frames, from Tropical Scorpio to Sidereal Libra at 15°51', and stays in the first house in both. In Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind god, you keep yourself movable: when a room starts to fix you in place, you shift your footing, adjust your terms, and leave yourself a way out before anyone notices you were looking for one.

### Debilitated in Libra, House 1

The Sun is debilitated here. The planet that wants to stand alone and be counted sits in the sign that measures everything against another person, so your will tends to form only after you have registered what the other side of an exchange requires. Being yourself in the body costs real effort. Asserting a preference without first checking its effect takes a deliberate push that someone with a Leo or Aries Sun would not need, and the cost is a standing tiredness in being the one who decides. One classical cancellation condition is met: Mars, lord of Aries, where the Sun is exalted, sits in a kendra from the Moon. That condition is named in the texts, and it does not remove the fall.

The Sun rules the eleventh house, so gains, networks, elder siblings and the things you hope to collect over time arrive in the first house as matters of your own body and presence. Whether a goal advances tends to register physically, as your own standing rising or sinking. Swati adds what the sign alone does not show. Your steadiness depends on mobility, and you hold a centre best when nothing pins it.

### What Sits Beside It

Mercury, retrograde, and Pluto share Libra with the Sun. Your sense of identity therefore forms alongside a mind that reviews its own conclusions and a pressure that will not let a settled self stay settled. This is where your capacity lives: in a first meeting or a fresh negotiation, you can hold a calm centre and offer warmth that is plainly yours, without performing it. The same conjunction is why that calm is so quickly reopened, and you replay the meeting afterwards to check what you gave away.

The Sun casts its drishti on the seventh house, Aries, which is empty. Your attention goes out toward partners and open opponents and finds no planet there to answer it. The pattern is live in the first house whenever another person's terms meet yours: introductions, pairings, the moment a group decides who leads. It goes quiet when you are alone or moving, which is Vayu's condition, because your identity has room to circulate when no one is asking it to commit.

The Ketu mahadasha runs until 2027-10, and the Mercury antardasha has just begun inside it. Ketu loosens attachments to outcome, and Mercury is the planet sitting in the first house with this Sun. The sub-period therefore brings the retrograde reviewing to the front of how you carry yourself in these months. Eleventh-house hopes are likely to come up for reconsideration through your own body and presence, under a dasha that keeps asking how much of them you still want.
