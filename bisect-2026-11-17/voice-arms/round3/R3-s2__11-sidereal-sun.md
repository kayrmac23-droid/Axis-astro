## The Sun

Your Sun moves back one sign in this frame, from Scorpio to Libra at 15°51', and it stays in the first house in both charts. In Swati, the nakshatra ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind, you keep yourself movable. When a situation tightens around you, you shift your footing, change the terms, find the angle that lets you keep going, and you treat staying in motion as the condition of staying yourself. Libra alone would give you the instinct to balance. Swati adds the need for room to sway while you do it, and the shadow is the same flexibility under strain: you bend so readily that a position can drift before you notice it has moved.

### The Sun in Libra, House 1

In Jyotish the Sun is debilitated in Libra. Read plainly, your will has to work through a sign organised around the other person's response, so asserting yourself costs more effort than it should and often lands at lower volume than you meant. Because this sits in the first house, the cost runs through the body and your instinctive approach to anything new. You feel your vitality rise when terms are fair and drain when they are not, and you can spend real energy settling an exchange before you have said what you came to say. That expense stays with you, and the chart offers nothing that cancels it.

One classical condition tempers the debilitation. Mars, lord of Aries where the Sun is exalted, stands in a kendra from your Moon. When you have to act alone and out of sight, a steadier drive is available to you there, and you can follow through on a decision without waiting for agreement. It eases the fall without lifting it.

The Sun rules your eleventh house and brings it into the first. What you hope for, and the circles you belong to, attach to your own person, so belonging feels like something you embody and must keep earning in your own conduct, not something a group confers on you. The Sun's own sight falls on the seventh, Aries, which holds no planets. Your attention turns towards the open, direct partner, and nothing in the chart stands there to meet it.

### Conjunctions

Mercury and Pluto share the sign. Pluto sits within half a degree, so the will and its intensity move as one weight. When you commit to something, the commitment carries more force than your debilitated Sun alone could supply, and it is just as hard to set down. Mercury, retrograde, sits further back in Libra, which means your self-assertion goes through a second pass of thought before it leaves you. You revise what you meant while the moment is still open.

### Timing

Your Ketu mahadasha runs until late 2027, and its Mercury sub-period has only just begun. Ketu loosens your grip on outcomes, and Mercury, conjunct this Sun in your first house, is now the planet in front. You are reviewing how you present yourself and what you are willing to stand behind in person. The retrograde thinking is the part being worked. Each settlement you reach about who you are gets reopened and examined again, while the release of the larger cycle makes it easier to let a stance go than to defend it.
