## Where They Part

Mars carries the widest gap between the two charts, because in the Tropical frame it rules the Ascendant itself, so whatever happens to this planet happens to the instinctive posture of the whole person. The other three load-bearing shifts, the Ascendant, the Sun and Jupiter, each run back through Mars or answer it.

### Mars: Libra in Detriment, Virgo Peregrine

The Tropical Mars sits at Libra 15°56' in the twelfth house, in detriment, and its dispositor is Venus in Scorpio in the first, also in detriment. Two compromised planets pass the will between them. The pattern this produces is assertion converted into negotiation: this person feels the push to act and routes it into weighing what would be fair, out of view, until the push arrives in the room as a proposal.

The Sidereal Mars sits at Virgo 22°15', still in the twelfth, peregrine, in Hasta Pada 4, with Mercury in Libra in the first, peregrine and retrograde, as its dispositor. Neither planet in this chain is debilitated, and the Jyotish account of the same withheld force is craft. Mars works by correcting a thing until it holds, and Mercury, turning back over its own ground, keeps reopening the draft.

The collision is set off by the Mars-Saturn sextile, 2.8° and applying, which ties Mars to Saturn in the second house in both charts. When a commitment that touches what this person owns or has promised comes under pressure, both frames mobilise Mars, and they mobilise it toward different ends. Under the Tropical account they work out terms both sides could accept and offer them; the dispute ends, and they are left with a settlement they arranged and do not quite feel entitled to. Under the Sidereal account they go back to the work itself and fix it until nobody can fault it; the fault is gone, and the grievance that started it was never spoken. The Tropical chain is under more pressure, with detriment at both links. The Sidereal chain is freer and more solitary, because what it produces answers to a standard only this person holds.

### The Ascendant: Scorpio and Libra

The Tropical Ascendant is Scorpio at 6°57', fixed water, and the threshold instinct it gives is probing: a new room is read for what it hides. The Sidereal Lagna is Libra at 13°16', cardinal air, ruled by Venus in detriment in the second, and the same threshold becomes a question of terms. The element changes, water to air, and so does the mode, fixed to cardinal.

The two charts disagree about what a first meeting is for. In the Tropical frame it is reconnaissance, and this person leaves knowing who carries the undercurrent and having said little. In the Sidereal frame it is the opening of an exchange, and they leave having set its terms, uneasy until those terms hold. Both describe one person crossing one threshold, and each account leaves out what the other one notices.

### The Sun: Scorpio Peregrine, Libra in Fall

The Tropical Sun is Scorpio 9°32' in the first, peregrine, disposited by that detriment Mars. The Sidereal Sun is Libra 15°51' in the first, in fall, in Swati, disposited by Venus in Scorpio in detriment. Here the Sidereal account carries the heavier load: fall, with a ruler in detriment.

The Sun-Pluto conjunction, 0.4° and separating, is the trigger, and it is one relationship read twice. Pluto sits in Scorpio in the Tropical chart and in Libra in the Sidereal. When someone's offhand remark lands wrong, the Tropical account registers it as a breach of trust, felt in full, and this person goes quiet and holds it at that depth for days. The Sidereal account registers the same remark as a shift in standing. This person recalculates their claim against the other person's, concedes ground they had no need to concede, and is left with a self that waits for a verdict before it will settle. The first account leaves them carrying a wound. The second leaves them carrying a debt they did not incur.

### Jupiter: Aries Peregrine, Pisces Domicile

The Tropical Jupiter is Aries 22°43' in the sixth, peregrine and retrograde, ruled by detriment Mars in the twelfth. The Sidereal Jupiter is Pisces 29°02' in the sixth, in domicile and retrograde, in Revati, its own dispositor, forming Harsha Yoga. The Sidereal degree lies close to a sign boundary and the Tropical one does not; within its frame, the Sidereal Jupiter is fully in Pisces.

The Jupiter-Uranus trine, 1.6° and separating, sets this off whenever a routine stops working. Under the Tropical account this person scraps the routine and starts again, fired by Aries and backed by Uranus in the second, and is left with a fresh beginning whose follow-through depends on a Mars that defers. Under the Sidereal account they take in more of the routine and widen what they serve, with Uranus now in the third, and are left with competence that has absorbed the problem and set no limit to it. The pressure falls on the Tropical side: a peregrine Jupiter with a ruler in detriment meets the same moment as a Jupiter answering only to itself.

The divergence also runs, unwalked and unresolved, through Ketu, the Midheaven, Rahu, the Moon, Neptune, Mercury, Pluto, Saturn, Venus and Uranus, each a further point at which the two accounts assign this life a different address.

What this person is most likely to misread is the timing. One chart has them negotiate and the other has them correct; one feels the wound and the other recalculates the debt. Because both responses come from the same withheld Mars, the person cannot tell from the inside which account has just run. The result can look like hesitation. It is two complete answers arriving at once, and neither one cancels the other.
