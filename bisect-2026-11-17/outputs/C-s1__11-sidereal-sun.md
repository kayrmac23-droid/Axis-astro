## The Sun

The Sidereal Sun moves back one sign, from Tropical Scorpio 9°32' to Libra 15°51', and stays in the first house in both frames. It sits in Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind. When Swati is active, you keep your identity in motion: you shift position slightly in each exchange, bending toward the other person's terms so that nothing pins you to a single fixed stance. Libra alone would explain the wish for balance. Swati adds the need to keep moving, and you feel it as a refusal to be cornered into one definitive version of yourself.

### Sun in Libra, Debilitated, House 1

In Jyotish the Sun is debilitated in Libra, and in the first house that condition sits in the body and the instinctive approach itself. Self-assertion is the Sun's function, and Libra asks it to wait for the other side's reply first. The cost is plain. When you need to claim something for yourself outright, a decision, a preference, a right to take up room, the claim forms slowly and arrives qualified. You feel the effort it takes to say "I want this" without first measuring how it will land, and that effort does not lessen with practice.

The chart carries one classical neecha bhanga condition: Mars, lord of Aries, the Sun's exaltation sign, sits in a kendra from the Moon, in Virgo in the twelfth. Sources weigh this condition differently. It names a route by which the debilitation can be worked through. It does not erase it, and the route runs through a twelfth-house Mars, so whatever strength it lends gathers out of view.

### Lordship and Conjunctions

The Sun rules the eleventh house, Leo, and carries it into the first. Gains, networks and the circle you hope to belong to become matters of your own person. You tend to measure your standing in a group by how you yourself are received there, and a cool welcome from a circle registers on you as a verdict about you personally. The Sun's own drishti falls on the seventh house, Aries, which holds no tenants. The open partner's house it looks into is also the Sun's exaltation sign, and that is where a direct self-claim would come easily.

Mercury, retrograde, and Pluto share the sign with the Sun. Pluto, at 16°13', sits almost on the Sun and gives the bent, accommodating posture unusual pressure underneath. Mercury's retrograde motion means the words for what you want get rehearsed and revised before they leave you.

The pattern is live in first meetings, in negotiations over your own terms, and in group settings where your place has not yet been settled. It goes quiet where the terms are already fixed and fair, because Libra then has nothing left to weigh and the Sun can stand without asking permission. Your capacity here is real and specific. You can hold a centre that others steady around without forcing it on them, and that steadiness comes from adjusting gracefully to whoever is present.

The Ketu mahadasha runs until 2027-10, and the Mercury antardasha within it has only just begun. Mercury shares the Sun's sign and house, so this new sub-period brings the conjunct, retrograde planet into the foreground of the same first house the Sun occupies. Ketu loosens attachment to how you are received in the eleventh-house circles the Sun rules. Mercury sharpens the private rehearsal of every self-claim. Together they make this year a test of whether you can state what you want once, plainly, before the revising begins.
