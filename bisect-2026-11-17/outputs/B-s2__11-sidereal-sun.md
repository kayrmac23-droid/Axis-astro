## The Sun

Your Sun moves back one sign between the frames, from Tropical Scorpio to Sidereal Libra at 15°51', and it stays in the first house in both. In Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind god, it makes you change position the moment a stance starts to fix you in place. You concede the point, alter the angle and let the exchange carry you somewhere new, keeping your footing by refusing to be pinned. Libra alone would ask only for balance; Swati adds a need for room to move inside that balance, and it will leave even a fair arrangement once it begins to feel permanent.

### Sun in Libra, House 1, Debilitated

The Sun is debilitated in Libra, and the cost is concrete. The planet that wants to declare who you are sits in a sign that measures every declaration against the other person's response. In the first house this reaches the body and your instinctive approach, so you tend to state yourself only after you have checked how the exchange is balanced, and a self that waits for the scales to settle is rarely stated at full volume. Its dispositor, Venus in detriment in Scorpio in the second, gives no relief, because the Lagna lord is itself testing whatever it is offered before trusting it.

One classical cancellation condition is present. Mars, lord of Aries, where the Sun is exalted, sits in a kendra from the Moon, in Virgo in the twelfth. Sources weigh this condition differently. The effort the debilitation asks of you to be counted as yourself remains, and nothing elsewhere in this frame pays it back.

### Lordship and Conjunctions

The Sun rules the eleventh house, Leo, and carries it into the first. Your gains, your friendships and the groups you belong to are therefore lived through your own body and presence. Your need to matter attaches to the circle around you, and you register your standing in it physically, as ease or strain in how you hold yourself among peers. Where the terms between you are even, this Sun gives you a steady, unforced presence, generous and free of performance; where they tilt, the debilitation shows first in your posture.

Mercury, retrograde, and Pluto share the Sun's sign. Mercury sends your statements about yourself back for revision before they leave you, so they have usually been reworded twice. Pluto, which the Tropical reading set beside a Scorpio Sun, here gives the same weight to a Libran identity, so even your concessions land heavily. The Sun's drishti falls on the seventh house, Aries, which holds no planet, and the balance you look for there has to be struck with whoever arrives.

This pattern is live wherever your standing has to be set in front of people whose agreement you count on, and the eleventh-house lordship places that among friends and peers. It goes quiet when you are moving and nothing asks you to fix a position, the condition Swati names. Free to change direction, you stop checking the scales, and your presence returns to its own register.

You are in the Ketu mahadasha until 2027-10, and the Mercury antardasha has just begun within it. Mercury is the planet sharing the Sun's sign and house, so this sub-period brings the revising, retrograde voice into the foreground beside your identity, inside a period that loosens your hold on positions you once took. For now, stating who you are tends to run through second drafts, and Ketu's release makes it easier to drop an old description of yourself than to finish a new one. What stays open is the version of yourself you have not yet put your name to among the people the eleventh house gathers.
