## Where They Part

The two charts agree on what this person feels and how they weigh. They disagree on how this person acts, what they lead with, what their identity is made of, and where growth goes. Those four places are where the frames give separate accounts of the same life, and each account carries its own evidence.

### Mars: Libra in Detriment, Virgo Peregrine

In the Tropical chart Mars sits at 15°56' Libra in the twelfth house, in detriment, and its dispositor is Venus in Scorpio, also in detriment. Two compromised planets govern one another here. The will this produces brokers. It takes a grievance somewhere private and returns with terms both parties can accept, and the Mars section traced that softening from accusation to question.

In the Sidereal chart the same Mars sits at 22°15' Virgo, still in the twelfth, peregrine, in Hasta. Its dispositor is Mercury in Libra in the first house, peregrine and retrograde. This will corrects. It takes the grievance away to refine it until it is precise enough to defend.

The Mars sextile Saturn (2.8°, applying) sets the two accounts in motion at the same moment. A commitment this person has made starts to fail: an obligation is not being met, or an agreed arrangement is slipping. Under the Tropical account they withdraw to find the version of the complaint the other party can tolerate. They return holding an agreement, and the original want has been negotiated down. Under the Sidereal account they withdraw to make the complaint exact. They return holding a case that is correct in every particular and still has not been said aloud, because the draft can always be improved once more.

The pressure is uneven. The Tropical Mars is compromised by sign and by ruler. The Sidereal Mars carries no detriment, and its ruler is unhindered by sign. Agreement and accuracy are two different settlements of one deferred act, and the life holds both.

### The Ascendant: Scorpio 6°57', Libra 13°16'

The Tropical Ascendant is fixed water. This person crosses a threshold already probing for what is hidden, with the Sun, Venus and Pluto all in the first house to thicken that stance. Its ruler is the detriment Mars.

The Sidereal Lagna is cardinal air, Libra. It is ruled by Venus in Scorpio in the second house, in detriment, joined by Saturn in a Raja Yoga between the lords of the first and fifth houses. Under this account life organises itself around negotiation, pairing and the visible terms on which an exchange settles.

Consider a first meeting where the arrangement is not yet clear. Under the Scorpio account this person reads for concealment and says little. They leave with a private verdict on who could be trusted. Under the Libra account they initiate. They propose the terms, find the pairing, and move the exchange toward something even. They leave with an agreement they still have to test through a Saturn-weighted Venus before they will believe it. In both frames the ruler sits in detriment, so both accounts carry comparable strain, and each one asks something of the threshold that the other does not.

### The Sun: Scorpio Peregrine, Libra in Fall

The Tropical Sun sits at 9°32' Scorpio, peregrine. Identity there is depth, intensity held under a lid. The Sidereal Sun sits at 15°51' Libra, debilitated, in Swati, ruled by Rahu. Identity there is calibration: selfhood checked against other people's standing before it is claimed. The Sun-Pluto conjunction (0.4°) is one relationship, read through two signs.

Something small shifts: a remark lands carelessly, or someone's attention moves elsewhere. Under the Scorpio account the conjunction registers this at a depth the event did not earn, and this person carries an intensity they cannot discharge. Under the Libra account the same conjunction presses on a Sun already working to stand upright, and this person reopens the question of where they stand. They are left with the matter unsettled, and their claim to it has been deferred until others weigh in.

Here the Sidereal account carries more strain. A Sun in fall pays an assertion cost that the peregrine Scorpio Sun does not, although the Sidereal frame records the classical cancellation through Mars's angularity from the Moon.

### Jupiter: Aries Peregrine, Pisces Domicile

Both frames place Jupiter in the sixth house, retrograde. In the Tropical chart it sits at 22°43' Aries, peregrine, ruled by the detriment Mars. In the Sidereal chart it sits at 29°02' Pisces in its own sign, ruling itself, in Revati, and it forms a Harsha Yoga. This Sidereal degree lies within 3° of the sign's end, and the Tropical degree does not. Within its own frame the Pisces Jupiter is fully in Pisces.

The Jupiter-Uranus trine (1.6°) sets this off: an unscheduled break appears in the daily routine. Under the Aries account this person treats the break as a licence and launches the improved regimen at once. They are left with a fresh start whose follow-through depends on a Mars that acts late. Under the Pisces account they absorb the disruption into the routine and widen what the routine will contain. They are left with more scope and a workload with no visible edge. The Tropical Jupiter is under more pressure, peregrine and ruled by a compromised planet. The Sidereal Jupiter answers only to itself.

Beyond these four, the charts also part across Ketu, the Midheaven, Rahu, the Moon, Neptune, Mercury, Pluto, Saturn, Venus and Uranus, and each of those remains open between the two accounts.

What this person is most likely to misread is the source of their own inconsistency. When they broker one week and correct the next, or launch one month and absorb the next, it can look like indecision about a single self. The chart shows two complete accounts of how this person acts and grows, each internally coherent. The cost is that neither account can be used to audit the other, so when one settlement leaves something undone, the other frame's way of doing it reads to this person as the thing they failed to do.
