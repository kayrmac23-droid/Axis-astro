## Where They Part

### Mars: Libra in the Tropical Twelfth, Virgo in the Sidereal Twelfth

Mars keeps the twelfth house in both charts and changes almost everything else. In the Tropical chart it sits at 15°56' Libra in detriment and rules the Scorpio Ascendant. Its dispositor is Venus in Scorpio in the first, also in detriment. The result is a will that has to clear a judgement of fairness before it moves, and the judge is compromised too, since two detriment planets each depend on the other for what it lacks.

In the Sidereal chart Mars sits at 22°15' Virgo in Hasta, peregrine. Its dispositor is Mercury, peregrine and retrograde in Libra in the first. Here Mars rules the second and seventh houses, so what this person holds and whom they openly face are both settled by a will that works by correcting a draft. The Tropical account carries the heavier load: detriment disposited by detriment, where the Sidereal Mars carries no detriment and answers to a dispositor whose difficulty is retrogradation alone.

The close sextile to Saturn, at 2.8° and applying, is where the two accounts produce different acts. Saturn holds the second house in both frames, Sagittarius in the Tropical and Scorpio in the Sidereal. The sextile switches on when a commitment already in force bears on what this person owns or is owed. Under the Tropical account they go quiet and test whether the other party's terms are honest and whether pressing their own claim would be fair. The step they take is a balanced one, and the suspicion that prompted the test keeps running after the matter has closed.

Under the Sidereal account they rework their own position until every clause of it is defensible, and what they bring forward is exact and finished. The cost is that the standard it was measured against is theirs alone. The other party may never learn there was a dispute to settle.

### The Ascendant: Scorpio at 6°57', Libra at 13°16'

The rising sign shifts element, from fixed water to cardinal air. Scorpio rising enters a room probing for what is concealed. Libra rising enters it measuring whether the terms between people have settled. The ruler chain makes this sharper than a change of sign. The Tropical chart ruler is Mars in Libra and the Sidereal Lagna lord is Venus in Scorpio, so each frame's ruler of the self sits in the sign the other frame rises in, and both rulers are in detriment. Neither account has a steady ruler to lean on.

The moment this surfaces is a first meeting in which someone offers easy agreement. The Scorpio Ascendant registers the agreement as a surface and starts reading beneath it. The Libra Lagna registers it as balance reached and lets the exchange proceed on those terms. This person tends to do the second out loud while the first continues underneath, and they leave having accepted terms they are still checking.

### The Sun: Scorpio Peregrine, Libra Debilitated

In the Tropical chart the Sun sits at 9°32' Scorpio in the first house, peregrine, conjunct Pluto at 0.4°, ruled by Mars in detriment. In the Sidereal chart it sits at 15°51' Libra in the first, in Swati, debilitated, conjunct a Pluto now also in Libra, ruled by Venus in detriment. The conjunction is one relationship read through two frames. In Scorpio it doubles a self that knows what it wants and carries the want with weight. In Libra it presses Pluto's force onto a self that will not claim its standing until it has weighed everyone else's. The Sidereal Sun is under the heavier dignity pressure, while the Tropical Sun has the steadier sign and the more compromised ruler.

When this person's position is being settled among a group, the accounts part. Under the Tropical Sun they hold the decision at full gravity, read every motive present, and come away with a position they feel completely and have barely stated. Under the Sidereal Sun, which rules the eleventh house of networks from the Libra Lagna, they defer their claim until the group's balance is clear, and come away with standing granted on terms they did not set. The close sextile to Neptune, in Capricorn in one frame and Sagittarius in the other, eases neither outcome.

### Jupiter: Aries Peregrine, Pisces in Its Own Sign

Jupiter is retrograde in the sixth in both frames. In the Tropical chart it sits at 22°43' Aries, peregrine, disposited by Mars in detriment in the twelfth. That gives an appetite to restart the routine whose execution depends on a will that defers. In the Sidereal chart it sits at 29°02' Pisces in Revati, in its own sign, ruling the third and sixth, which forms Harsha Yoga. The Sidereal degree lies near the end of its sign and the Tropical one is clear of any boundary; that is a difference of position between the frames, and in its own frame this Jupiter is fully Pisces. The Tropical Jupiter is under more pressure, its delivery tied to Mars's detriment, while the Sidereal Jupiter answers only to itself.

The tight trine to Uranus, at 1.6° and separating, supplies the trigger: a break in the daily pattern, with the body or the schedule disrupted from outside. Under the Tropical account this person takes the break as a starting signal and plans the overhaul. Then they wait on a will that has not moved, holding a better routine that exists only in outline. Under the Sidereal account they absorb the disruption into the routine and put it to use, a sixth-house difficulty turned into competence inside its own domain. They come out more capable and more heavily loaded than before.

The divergence continues, unwalked, across Ketu, the Midheaven, Rahu, the Moon, Neptune, Mercury, Pluto, Saturn, Venus and Uranus, each a further point where the two accounts give the same life a different address.

The tension this person lives inside is the crossed rulership at the threshold of the self. The Tropical self is governed by a Mars in Libra and the Sidereal self by a Venus in Scorpio, so whichever account they act from, its ruler stands in the other account's rising sign. When they move on the depth-probe, the Libra Mars asks whether the move is fair. When they move on the measure of balance, the Scorpio Venus asks what the balance is hiding. They are likely to call this indecision. The chart shows two complete accounts, each governed by a ruler in detriment, and the cost is that no stance this person takes at a threshold arrives unquestioned by its own ruler.
