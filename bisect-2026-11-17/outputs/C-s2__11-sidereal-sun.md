## The Sun

The Sun moves back one sign between the frames, from Tropical Scorpio at 9°32' to Sidereal Libra at 15°51', and stays in the first house in both. In Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind god, your first move when a situation starts to close around you is to shift your footing: you change the terms, take a step sideways, keep yourself able to leave. Libra alone would explain the weighing. Swati adds the need to keep moving while you weigh, so that agreeing to something never quite becomes being fixed by it.

### Sun in Libra, House 1, Debilitated

In Jyotish the Sun is debilitated in Libra, and the cost is plain. The planet of self-assertion sits in a sign that measures every position against someone else's, so your own claim to be the one who decides arrives late and qualified. In the first house this is the condition of the body and of how you meet your life. When you need to put yourself forward without a partner, a counterweight or someone to answer, the solar will has little to stand on, and you work harder for a plain statement of who you are than the moment seems to require. The dispositor is Venus in Scorpio in the second, the Lagna lord whose testing of resources the Lagna section has already traced. Your self-assertion therefore depends on a planet that will not trust what it holds until it has weighed it.

One classical cancellation condition is met: Mars, lord of Aries where the Sun is exalted, stands in a kendra from the Moon. Sources weigh this differently. It names a route by which the debilitation can be worked against. The debilitation remains.

### Lordship and Company

The Sun rules the eleventh, Leo, and carries it into the first. Gains, networks and the future you are aiming at get lived through your own body and presence. When the eleventh-house aims are live, such as a group forming or a goal coming within reach, the Sun's pattern is most visible: you set yourself up as the balancing point of the group, and the debilitated will tends to bargain away its claim to lead.

Mercury and Pluto share the sign. Retrograde Mercury in Chitra keeps turning your self-account over, and Pluto in Swati at 16°13', close to the Sun's own degree, presses that same restless movement toward intensity. The Sun casts its drishti on the seventh, Aries, which holds no planets. Your will points across at the partner's house and finds it empty, so the counterweight Libra needs is something you go and look for. Nothing in the chart supplies one already in place.

The pattern goes quiet when no one asks you to stand alone. Inside a pairing or a shared aim, the Libra Sun functions well, and you can be the steady, generous centre of the exchange with the terms settled around you.

The Mercury antardasha has only just begun within the Ketu mahadasha, and Mercury is one of the Sun's own companions in the first house. For the coming year the retrograde reconsidering that shares the Sun's sign comes into the foreground, while Ketu loosens your hold on the eleventh-house aims the Sun carries. You are likely to find your account of yourself under review at a time when the goals that usually give it shape are easing their grip. The Swati need to keep your footing free meets very little that holds it in place during this period.
