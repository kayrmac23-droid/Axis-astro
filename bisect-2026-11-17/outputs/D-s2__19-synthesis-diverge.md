## Where They Part

Mars carries the widest shift in this chart, because it governs the Tropical Ascendant and changes sign, dignity and dispositor all at once. The Tropical Mars sits at Libra 15°56' in the twelfth house, in detriment, ruled by a Venus in Scorpio that is also in detriment. The Sidereal Mars sits at Virgo 22°15' in the twelfth house, peregrine, in Hasta, ruled by a retrograde Mercury in Libra in the first house, also peregrine. The house holds. The method of the withheld will does not.

### Mars: Libra Against Virgo

In the Tropical account, this person's drive is asked to work through a sign built for agreement. A want arrives, and the first move is to test it against the other party's likely response until it can be offered as something both sides could accept. Because the dispositor is itself in detriment, nothing upstream steadies that negotiation, and the pressure compounds.

In the Sidereal account, Mars is under less strain. Virgo hands it a craft. The want arrives, and the first move is to correct it, refining the complaint until it can survive any objection. Mercury, its dispositor, is unencumbered by detriment, so the chain runs cleaner. On dignity alone, the Tropical Mars carries more pressure.

The Mars-Saturn sextile, 2.8° and applying, is where the two accounts lead to different acts. When a commitment that touches their resources comes under strain, the Tropical Mars responds by seeking terms. It offers a revised arrangement that keeps the peace, and they are left with an agreement they accepted and a grievance that was never put on the table. The Sidereal Mars responds to the same strain by producing an exact, defensible account of what went wrong. It is complete and correct, and it is often never sent, so they are left holding a case nobody has heard. The wide opposition to Jupiter adds a thin third pressure to both.

### The Ascendant: Scorpio Against Libra

The Tropical Ascendant rises at Scorpio 6°57': fixed water, ruled by that detriment Mars. Its threshold instinct is to probe. On meeting someone, this person reads for concealment and holds the reading close. The Sidereal Lagna rises at Libra 13°16': cardinal air, with Venus as Lagna lord, in detriment in Scorpio in the second house. In this frame, the circumstances that form around them keep turning into negotiations over terms, pairing and settlement.

The two accounts meet in the same opening minutes of a first meeting. Under the Tropical reading, the question this person carries into that encounter is what the other person is withholding. Under the Sidereal reading, the question is what the exchange between them is going to cost and whether it can be made even. Both questions run on the same first handshake, with different evidence behind each. The pressure also falls differently. The Tropical ruler is in detriment in a hidden house, while the Sidereal ruler is in detriment in the house of resources. One account strains at disclosure, the other at what can be kept.

### The Sun: Scorpio Against Libra

The Tropical Sun stands at Scorpio 9°32' in the first house, peregrine, ruled by Mars in detriment. Identity runs at depth, and the Pluto conjunction, 0.4° and separating, compounds that depth. The Sidereal Sun stands at Libra 15°51' in the first house, debilitated, in Swati, ruled by Venus in detriment. Here identity has to be claimed against an instinct that keeps checking its standing against everyone else's. By dignity, the Sidereal Sun is under the greater strain.

The conjunction with Pluto sets the scene. A careless remark lands, and it registers far below the size of what was said. Under the Tropical account, this person absorbs it as a breach. They go still, mark the speaker, and are left carrying a recalibrated trust that the remark never earned. Under the Sidereal account, the same remark tips a balance. They spend the evening deciding whether they are entitled to mind, and they are left with their own standing still under review. The Neptune sextile, 3.7°, offers an imaginative way out in both frames. It is used only when something opens it.

### Jupiter: Aries Against Pisces

The Tropical Jupiter sits at Aries 22°43' in the sixth house, peregrine and retrograde, ruled by detriment Mars. It wants to launch the improvement before the old routine has failed. The Sidereal Jupiter sits at Pisces 29°02' in the sixth house, in its own sign and retrograde, in Revati, and forms Harsha Yoga. It takes in the whole of a routine and confines difficulty to work and the body. That position lies close to the end of Pisces in the Sidereal frame and well inside Aries in the Tropical. Within its own frame, each is fully in its sign. The Tropical Jupiter is the one under pressure, since the Sidereal Jupiter disposits itself.

The Jupiter-Uranus trine, tight at 1.6°, triggers the split. When the daily routine breaks without warning, the Tropical account starts a new regimen that week and is left with something begun before anyone knew it was needed. The Sidereal account absorbs the break into the existing work and carries more of it. It is left with competence that has grown, and with no clear point at which the load became too much. The Saturn trine lets each version settle quickly enough that the question of which one served them goes unasked.

The divergence continues, unwalked and still open, across Ketu, the Midheaven, Rahu, the Moon, Neptune, Mercury, Pluto, Saturn, Venus and Uranus.

What this person lives inside is a single will with two methods of delay. In the Tropical frame the will waits to be agreed with; in the Sidereal frame it waits to be correct. Both reach the room late, and from the inside each feels like conscientiousness. The cost is that neither frame tells them which kind of lateness happened on a given day. Each time the moment passes, they cannot say whether they held back to keep the peace or to get the case right. That uncertainty is the part of the divergence they carry unexamined.
