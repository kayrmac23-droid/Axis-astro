## The Sun

The Sun moves back one sign between the frames, from Tropical Scorpio to Sidereal Libra at 15°51', and stays in the first house in both. Its nakshatra is Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the wind. When Swati is active you protect your capacity to move: you keep a position flexible enough that you can leave it without loss, and you bend toward whoever is pressing before you would let the exchange lock you in place.

### The Sun in Libra, Debilitated

In Jyotish the Sun is debilitated in Libra. The planet of self-authorisation sits in the sign that measures every act against how it lands on the other side of the pair. The cost is concrete. In the first house, which here means your body, your bearing and your instinctive approach, you find it hard to put weight behind a preference until you have checked that it is fair. By the time you have checked, the moment for asserting it has often passed. Swati deepens this. Vayu's wind moves around obstacles and does not plant itself, so the self you present tends to stay provisional, and committing to it outright tends to feel like losing the freedom to adjust.

One classical cancellation condition is met. Mars, lord of Aries where the Sun is exalted, sits in a kendra from the Moon, in Virgo in the twelfth. Sources weigh this differently. It gives the debilitation a route toward function, and that route runs through the hidden twelfth-house Mars, so whatever steadiness it lends is built in private and does not show at the moment you need it.

### Lordship and Conjunctions

The Sun rules the eleventh house, Leo, and carries it into the first. Your gains, your networks and what you hope for become matters of personal identity. A circle's approval and a future plan register in you as questions about who you are, and when a group shifts its regard, you feel it in your bearing first.

Mercury and Pluto share Libra with the Sun. Mercury, retrograde, turns your self-presentation back on itself, so you reconsider a stance after taking it. Pluto, a few minutes past the Sun, charges the same first house with a pressure that the Libran wish for balance has to absorb. The Sun casts its drishti onto an empty seventh house, Aries. Nothing there answers it, and so the partner who might take up the assertion the Sun cannot make is not supplied by the chart.

### When It Is Live and When It Goes Quiet

This pattern is live wherever your standing within a group or a plan for the future is in question, because those are the eleventh-house matters the Sun carries into your body and bearing. It goes quiet when nothing in your circle is being decided and no direction is being asked of you. Then Swati's mobility costs you nothing, and you carry yourself without having to authorise anything.

The Mercury antardasha has just begun within the Ketu mahadasha, and Mercury sits in the same sign as this Sun. For the coming year, the sub-period lord shares the Sun's own house, and that brings the retrograde habit of revising your stance into the place where your identity is formed. Ketu's mahadasha already loosens your hold on what you have claimed. Under Mercury, the eleventh-house questions of which circle you belong to and which aim you are working toward are likely to come up as matters of self-definition. You meet them with a debilitated Sun that bends easily and authorises slowly, and you weigh each answer again in the first house, where your bearing shows the doubt before you have spoken it.
