## The Sun

In the Sidereal frame your Sun moves back one sign, from Tropical Scorpio 9°32' to Libra 15°51', and it holds the first house in both frames. It falls in Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu. When Swati is active, you keep your footing by staying movable: you shift position as the exchange shifts, adjust your stance to whoever is opposite, and leave yourself a way out. Being pinned in place, by a role or by someone else's fixed definition of you, registers first as a constriction in the body and only later as a disagreement.

### The Debilitated Sun in Libra, House 1

Libra is the Sun's sign of debilitation, and in the first house that difficulty lives in your body and your persona. The Sun's task is to stand as itself. Libra measures every stance against a counterpart, so your claim to be someone in particular tends to wait for a counterweight before it feels legitimate. Self-assertion costs you effort at the moment it is needed, and much of that effort goes into checking that a position is balanced before you allow yourself to hold it.

The ruler gives no relief. Libra is governed by Venus in Scorpio in the second, the same Venus that tests every resource before trusting it, so whatever authority the Sun lacks is not supplied by its dispositor. One classical cancellation condition is met: Mars, lord of Aries where the Sun is exalted, sits in Virgo in the twelfth, a kendra from the Moon. Sources weigh this differently. It qualifies the debilitation without removing it, and because Mars works from the twelfth, any lift it brings is earned privately and stays out of sight.

Swati gives this Sun a working capacity. You stay recognisably yourself across very different rooms by giving ground on the surface while keeping a centre you can return to. Under sustained pressure the bending can continue until there is little centre left, and you tend to discover that you have agreed your way out of a view you held an hour earlier.

### Lordship, Conjunctions and the Empty Seventh

The Sun rules the eleventh house, Leo, and carries it into the first. Your gains, your circles and the future you are building attach to your own person, so the groups you belong to form around you directly, and they carry the same uncertainty about standing that the debilitation imposes.

Mercury, retrograde at 5°07', shares Libra with the Sun. Your identity is therefore worked out in speech: you discover who you are by saying it, and the retrograde sends you back over what you said. Pluto also shares the sign, at 16°13', so the compression the Tropical portrait gave this Sun is present here as well. In this frame it presses on a self that wants its weight distributed evenly.

No drishti reaches the Sun. Its own seventh-house gaze falls on Aries, which holds no planets, so the counterweight Libra needs comes from whoever happens to occupy the partner or opponent seat. The pattern is live whenever your standing depends on a counterpart's response. It goes quiet when you are moving between positions and nobody's definition of you is being pressed.

The Ketu mahadasha, running until 2027-10, loosens your grip on positions; Ketu sits in Virgo in the twelfth. The Mercury antardasha has only just begun, about six weeks ago, and Mercury is the Sun's own companion in the first house. For this year the debilitated Sun's work runs through what you say about yourself. You state a position, the retrograde returns you to revise it, and the mahadasha makes holding it harder than letting it go.
