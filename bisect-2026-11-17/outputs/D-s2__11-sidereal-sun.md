## The Sun

The Sun moves back one sign between the frames, from Tropical Scorpio to Sidereal Libra at 15°51', and stays in the first house in both. In Swati, ruled by Rahu and presided over by Vayu, the god of wind, it gives you an instinct to shift position whenever a situation starts to fix you in place. You change the subject, reframe your stance, or step sideways out of a role before it hardens around you, and you feel steadier for having moved.

### Libra, Debilitated

In Jyotish the Sun is debilitated in Libra, and the cost is concrete. The planet of self-assertion sits in a sign that measures every move by its effect on the other party. In the first house that measuring runs through your body and your instinctive approach. You tend to check how a stance lands before you have finished taking it, and your own claim to authority arrives late or not at all. Its dispositor, Venus in Scorpio in the second, is itself in detriment, so the planet meant to steady this Sun is busy testing what anything is worth. A steady centre is available to you, and you reach it by deliberate effort that others in your position do not have to spend.

One classical cancellation condition is present: Mars, lord of Aries where the Sun is exalted, sits in a kendra from the Moon. Sources weigh it differently, and it does not erase the debilitation. It means the weakness has a counterweight somewhere in the chart, without saying when that counterweight engages.

### Lordship and Conjunctions

The Sun rules the eleventh house, Leo, and carries it into the first. Gains, networks and the circle you belong to become matters of personal identity. You measure yourself through the groups you are part of, and when one of them shifts or drops away, it registers in your body as a loss of footing.

Mercury and Pluto share the sign. Mercury is retrograde, so your conscious self is accompanied by a mind that goes back over what it has said and revises it. Pluto lends the same identity an intensity the Libran surface does not show. You present measured and even, while something denser keeps working under the presentation.

The Sun's drishti falls on the seventh house, Aries, which holds no planets. Your attention goes steadily toward partnership and open opposition, and nothing in that house answers it back. No graha casts drishti onto the Sun, so this pattern has no single outside trigger. It is live whenever your self-presentation is on the line: a first meeting, a negotiation over your own position, a group deciding where you stand. It goes quiet when you are alone and nothing about you is being weighed.

### The Current Chapter

The Mercury antardasha has just begun inside the Ketu mahadasha, and Mercury is the graha sitting beside this Sun in your first house. Ketu's long period loosens your hold on identity and eleventh-house affiliations. The new Mercury sub-period puts the retrograde, revising mind next to the Sun in charge of how that loosening is spoken about. Over the coming months you are likely to find yourself rewording who you are and which circles you belong to, often more than once, and each revision leaves the debilitated Sun one more draft away from a stance it will hold.
